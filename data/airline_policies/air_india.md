# Air India — Flight Rescheduling Policy

## Airline: Air India

## Rescheduling Rules

Air India allows passengers to reschedule their domestic flights subject to the following conditions:

### Eligibility
- All confirmed bookings are eligible for one date change.
- Cancelled bookings are not eligible for rescheduling.
- Rescheduling requests must be submitted at least 24 hours before the original departure time.

### Rescheduling Fee Structure
- **More than 7 days before departure**: Flat fee of ₹500.
- **3 to 7 days before departure**: Flat fee of ₹1,500.
- **24 hours to 3 days before departure**: Flat fee of ₹2,500.
- **Less than 24 hours before departure**: Rescheduling is not permitted.

### Fare Difference
- If the fare for the new travel date is higher than the original fare, the passenger must pay the fare difference in addition to the rescheduling fee.
- If the fare for the new travel date is lower, no refund of the fare difference is provided.

### Restrictions
- Maximum of two rescheduling requests per booking.
- Rescheduling is only available for the same route. Route changes are not permitted.
- The new travel date must be within 90 days of the original travel date.

### Special Conditions
- Premium and Business class tickets can be rescheduled with a 50% discount on the rescheduling fee.
- Passengers with medical emergencies may request a fee waiver by submitting valid medical documentation.

### Refund on Cancellation After Rescheduling
- If a passenger cancels after rescheduling, the rescheduling fee is non-refundable.
- Standard cancellation charges apply to the base fare.
