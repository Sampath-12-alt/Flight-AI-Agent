# Vistara — Flight Rescheduling Policy

## Airline: Vistara

## Rescheduling Rules

Vistara provides flexible rescheduling options for domestic flight bookings under the following conditions:

### Eligibility
- All confirmed and waitlisted bookings are eligible for date changes.
- Cancelled bookings are not eligible for rescheduling.
- Rescheduling requests must be submitted at least 4 hours before the original departure time.

### Rescheduling Fee Structure
- **More than 14 days before departure**: Flat fee of ₹250.
- **7 to 14 days before departure**: Flat fee of ₹1,000.
- **3 to 7 days before departure**: Flat fee of ₹2,000.
- **4 hours to 3 days before departure**: Flat fee of ₹3,000.
- **Less than 4 hours before departure**: Rescheduling is not permitted.

### Fare Difference
- If the fare on the new travel date is higher, the passenger must pay the difference along with the rescheduling fee.
- If the fare on the new travel date is lower, Vistara will issue the difference as a Vistara travel credit valid for 12 months.

### Restrictions
- A maximum of two date changes are permitted per booking.
- The new travel date must be within 120 days of the original departure date.
- Only date changes on the same route are allowed. Route modifications require a new booking.

### Fare Class Considerations
- **Economy Lite**: Subject to full rescheduling fee.
- **Economy Standard**: 25% discount on the rescheduling fee.
- **Economy Flex**: 50% discount on the rescheduling fee.
- **Premium Economy**: One complimentary date change, subsequent changes at 50% discount.
- **Business Class**: Two complimentary date changes, subsequent changes at 25% discount.

### Special Conditions
- Vistara Club Vistara Gold and Platinum members receive one complimentary date change per booking regardless of fare class.
- In case of schedule changes initiated by Vistara, passengers may reschedule without any fee.
- Medical emergencies require supporting documentation for fee waiver consideration.

### Post-Rescheduling Cancellation
- The rescheduling fee is non-refundable if the booking is subsequently cancelled.
- Cancellation charges are calculated based on the fare rules of the original booking.
