# SpiceJet — Flight Rescheduling Policy

## Airline: SpiceJet

## Rescheduling Rules

SpiceJet allows passengers to change their travel dates on domestic flights subject to the following terms:

### Eligibility
- Rescheduling is available for all confirmed bookings.
- Bookings with a status of Cancelled or Refunded are not eligible.
- The rescheduling request must be made at least 6 hours before the scheduled departure time.

### Rescheduling Fee Structure
- **More than 10 days before departure**: Flat fee of ₹500.
- **4 to 10 days before departure**: Flat fee of ₹1,250.
- **24 hours to 4 days before departure**: Flat fee of ₹2,000.
- **6 hours to 24 hours before departure**: Flat fee of ₹3,500.
- **Less than 6 hours before departure**: Rescheduling is not permitted.

### Fare Difference
- If the new travel date has a higher fare, the fare difference must be paid in addition to the rescheduling fee.
- If the new travel date has a lower fare, the fare difference is forfeited. No refund or credit is provided.

### Restrictions
- A maximum of three date changes are allowed per booking.
- Each rescheduling incurs the applicable fee at the time of the request.
- The new travel date must be within 45 days of the original departure date.
- Route changes are not permitted.

### Special Conditions
- SpiceMax bookings receive a 25% discount on the rescheduling fee.
- Group bookings (10 or more passengers) follow a separate rescheduling policy as per the group booking agreement.
- SpiceJet may waive rescheduling fees in the event of flight cancellations initiated by the airline.

### Refund on Cancellation After Rescheduling
- If a booking is cancelled after rescheduling, the rescheduling fee is non-refundable.
- The cancellation refund is calculated based on the original ticket price minus all applicable charges.
