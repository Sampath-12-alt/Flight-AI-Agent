# IndiGo — Flight Rescheduling Policy

## Airline: IndiGo

## Rescheduling Rules

IndiGo permits date changes on domestic flights under the following terms and conditions:

### Eligibility
- Only confirmed bookings are eligible for rescheduling.
- Cancelled or no-show bookings cannot be rescheduled.
- Changes must be requested at least 2 hours before the scheduled departure.

### Rescheduling Fee Structure
- **More than 15 days before departure**: ₹0 (free date change).
- **7 to 15 days before departure**: Flat fee of ₹750.
- **3 to 7 days before departure**: Flat fee of ₹1,750.
- **2 hours to 3 days before departure**: Flat fee of ₹3,000.
- **Less than 2 hours before departure**: Rescheduling is not permitted.

### Fare Difference
- Passengers are required to pay any fare difference if the new date has a higher fare.
- If the new date has a lower fare, the difference is provided as IndiGo credit, not as a cash refund.

### Restrictions
- Only one free rescheduling is allowed per booking (for changes made more than 15 days before departure).
- Subsequent changes are charged at the applicable fee tier.
- The new travel date must be within 60 days of the original date.
- Route changes are not allowed. Only date changes are permitted.

### Special Conditions
- IndiGo 6E Flex fares include one complimentary date change regardless of the time before departure.
- Corporate bookings may have different fee structures as per the corporate agreement.

### No-Show Policy
- If a passenger fails to board the original flight and has not requested a change, the booking is treated as a no-show and is not eligible for rescheduling.
